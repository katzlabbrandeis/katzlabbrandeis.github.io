The Katz Lab is an academic research lab at [Brandeis University](https://www.brandeis.edu/) in Waltham, Massachusetts, USA. 

We study the **neural ensemble dynamics of sensori-motor processes** in awake rodents, combining behavior, multi-neuronal electrophysiology, complex analysis and modeling, pharmacology and optogenetics to probe ongoing spiking activity in real-time.

Our research focuses on understanding how the brain processes sensory information and controls movement through the coordinated activity of neural ensembles.
