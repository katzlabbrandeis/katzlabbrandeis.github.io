# Research

## Neural Ensemble Dynamics

The Katz Lab studies the neural ensemble dynamics of sensori-motor processes in awake rodents. We combine multiple innovative methodologies:

- Behavioral analysis
- Multi-neuronal electrophysiology
- Complex data analysis and modeling
- Pharmacological interventions
- Optogenetic techniques

## Real-Time Analysis

Our lab specializes in real-time analysis of ongoing neural activity, providing insights into how the brain processes information and controls behavior in living organisms.

## Key Research Areas

### Gustatory Processing

We investigate how the brain processes taste information, including:
- Palatability coding in gustatory cortex
- Taste learning and memory
- Neural responses to taste stimuli

### Neural Ensemble Coordination

We study how groups of neurons work together to:
- Encode sensory information
- Generate motor commands
- Adapt to changing conditions

### Cortical Dynamics

Our research examines:
- Temporal evolution of cortical responses
- Ensemble coherence and coordination
- State-dependent processing
